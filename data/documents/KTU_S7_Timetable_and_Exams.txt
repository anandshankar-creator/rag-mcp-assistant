5. ATTENDANCE CRITERIA & CONDONATION RULES
- Minimum Attendance Requirement: KTU regulations mandate a minimum of 75% attendance
  in each individual subject to qualify for the End Semester University Examination.
- Condonation Window: Students having attendance between 65% and 74.9% may apply for
  condonation on genuine medical grounds by submitting medical certificates approved by
  the College Medical Officer and paying the KTU condonation fee of Rs. 1500 per subject.
- Below 65% Attendance: Not eligible for condonation; student will be awarded 'FE' (Failed
  due to lack of Eligibility) and must repeat the course.
